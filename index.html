Common questions

My prayers aren't on my other device. Use the same Apple ID on both and turn on iCloud for Hitherto in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Syncing can take a few minutes.

Someone appears twice. That can happen right after adding the same person on two devices; Hitherto merges them automatically the next time it opens.

Can others see my prayers? No. There are no accounts or shared lists. Turn on Face ID lock to keep the app and widget private on your phone.
