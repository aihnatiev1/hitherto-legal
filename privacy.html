Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Hitherto ("the app", "we") is a private prayer list. What you pray about, and for whom, is yours alone.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no servers. We never see your prayers, the names you add or anything else you write.

Where your prayers are stored

On your iPhone. If iCloud is on for the app, Apple keeps a copy in your private iCloud database, which only you can access; names, prayers and answer notes are encrypted by Apple before they leave your device.

Widget and reminders

The widget and the daily reminder are prepared on your iPhone. When Face ID lock is on, the widget shows only a count, never names. iCloud may use Apple's silent notifications to keep your devices in sync; we send no notifications ourselves.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app only learns from Apple whether you have Premium. We never receive your name, email or payment details.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous crash statistics. You control this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Your choices

  • Export: Prayers › Settings › Export my prayers creates a text file you can save or share.
  • Delete: Prayers › Settings › Delete all prayers removes everything from this iPhone and your iCloud.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
